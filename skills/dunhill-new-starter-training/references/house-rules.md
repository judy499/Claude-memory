# Dunhill house rules (apply to everything a new starter writes or changes)

Source: Judy's standing rules (CLAUDE.md, Nov 2026). Confirm with Esty or the trainer if the official training docs say something different, because the official docs win.

## Writing
- No em dashes. Keep prose concise.
- Customer emails: warm but professional, and not over-apologetic. Signed "Lesley".
- Copy must match the brand's voice (see Brands below).

## Listings
- Amazon is the single source of truth. Every other channel is corrected to match Amazon, never the other way round.
- Never invent or guess attribute values. Record the old value, the new value and the source for every change.
- Dietary claims, allergens and ingredients need extra care. If you're not sure, ask your trainer before changing anything.
- Walmart: keep the existing Walmart price (don't align it to Amazon). All Walmart listings carry the California Prop 65 warning.
- Target Plus: short titles that follow the title formula, 4 to 8 word bullets, 50+ word descriptions, no copy duplicated between bullets and description.
- TikTok product copy: a paragraph first, then bullets. No emoji and no special formatting.

## Brands
- Gourmanity: premium and globally sourced (not "European foods"). No olive oil. Kept separate from candy.
- 4YoreElves: whimsical and elfy.
- Spirits of Virtue: elevated and experiential ("drink differently").
- Magic Seasoning Blends: Cajun-Creole heritage (Chef Paul Prudhomme).

## Approvals
- TikTok Shop ads need Gayla's permission first.
- Ask before spending money (ads, samples, tools).
